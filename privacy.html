Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Mhat (“the app”) is a to-do app for iPhone, Mac and Windows. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: we only use what is needed to run the app, we never sell it, and there is no advertising or tracking.

Information you give the app

  • Your to-dos and lists — titles, notes, dates, reminders, projects and settings you create. These are stored on your device.
  • Your name — the display name you enter, used to greet you in the app.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app. We use these only to create your account and identify you, so your lists can sync between your devices. When you are signed in, your to-dos and settings are stored in our cloud database (provided by Supabase) so they appear on your other devices. We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. If you allow notifications, the app uses them only to remind you about your own to-dos.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools.
  • We never sell or rent your information, and we don’t share it except with the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for sync and sign-in, Google for sign-in).

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or remove the app. If you signed in, you can delete your account and all synced data at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account), or by emailing us and we will do it for you. We delete synced data when you delete your account.

Security

Data sent between the app and our cloud database is encrypted in transit, and each account can only access its own data.

Children

Mhat is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
